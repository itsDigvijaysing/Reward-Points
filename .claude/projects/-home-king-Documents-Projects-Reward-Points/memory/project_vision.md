User wants to rebuild Reward Points as an **anime-inspired RPG Status Window** — your real life IS the game.

**Four Pillars:**
1. **RPG System** — 6 stats (STR/INT/WIS/DEX/CHA/VIT) on hexagonal radar chart. Level + Rank (E→SSS). Stat decay for inactivity. Comeback bonus. RPG titles.
2. **Liquid Glass UI** — THE SYSTEM's interface. iOS 26 inspired glassmorphism over dark ambient bg.
3. **Todoist Integration** — Tasks mapped to stats. Priority-based XP. Auto-sync.
4. **Gemini AI Agent** — "THE SYSTEM" narrator. Evolving memory. YAML persona. RPG-aware.

**Why:** User wants a gamification app that feels alive — stats grow when you work, decay when you don't. Not a generic todo app but an RPG character sheet for real life.

**How to apply:** Every design decision should reinforce the RPG fantasy. The Status Window is the hero screen. Decay creates stakes. The AI speaks as the System.
